# Shared style preamble

The art direction (docs/SPEC.md › Art direction: grimdark casino), condensed. `## pixel` is prepended to every
pixel group's prompt, `## painted` to every painted group's; `## negative` is appended to every negative prompt.
A group file can replace the preamble with its own `## style` section.

## pixel

Pixel art for a grimdark casino party game. The House is a dead god and every casino is its church: casino
iconography made old, cruel and holy. Palette: black velvet, oxblood, tarnished brass, bone white, candle amber,
with {accentName} ({accent}) as the only bright colour, used sparingly. Hard pixels, 1-pixel dark outline,
two-step shading, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no glow, limited palette (under 32 colours).

## painted

Painterly grimdark casino illustration. The House is a dead god and every casino is its church: casino
iconography (chips, cards, dice, wheels, neon) made old, cruel and holy. Palette: black velvet, oxblood,
tarnished brass and gold, bone white, candle smoke and amber; one sickly neon accent, {accentName} ({accent}),
as the only bright thing in frame. Textured oil-and-ink feel, chiaroscuro, candlelight, no clean fantasy, nothing
cheerful.

## negative

text, letters, words, watermark, signature, logo, bright saturated fantasy colours, rainbow, cute, cartoon,
photograph, blurry, soft glow, bloom, lens flare, modern casino, Las Vegas skyline, extra limbs
