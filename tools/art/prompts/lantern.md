# lantern — the Health tell, 4-frame flicker per state (PixelLab)

## prompt

Pixel-art belt lantern, the Health readout of a grimdark casino game: a small brass-and-glass lantern seen from
the front, centred, 128×128, transparent background. State: {name}. The four states must be unmistakable at 24 px
by flame alone.

## variants

- full: Full flame: tall steady amber flame filling the glass, warm light on the brass.
- guttering: Guttering: a short, leaning, sputtering flame, half the glass dark, smoke curl.
- out: Almost out: a single tiny ember at the wick, glass dark, cold brass.
- dead: Dead: no flame, cracked glass, a thin wisp of grey smoke, the brass dull and blue-black.

## negative

background, hand, figure, text, glow outside the glass
