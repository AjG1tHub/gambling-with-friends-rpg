# wheels — rim textures for the wheel faces (FLUX.2, painted), pointer and hub (FLUX.2 → SVG wrap)

## prompt

{name} of a grimdark casino: the wheel is a breaking wheel. A painted, top-down circular rim and face texture in
tarnished gold, oxblood velvet and bone, worn and candle-lit, the centre and segments left as flat dark velvet
with no divisions, no labels and no numbers (the game draws the segments and labels over this). Perfectly centred
circle filling a square frame.

## variants

- pointer: A single wheel pointer: a bone dagger with a brass hilt pointing down, isolated on plain near-black. Tall 2:3.
- hub: A wheel hub: a round brass boss with a skull-stamped chip at its centre, isolated on plain near-black. Square.

## negative

segments, wedges, numbers, letters, text, labels, hands, people
