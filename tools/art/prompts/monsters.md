# monsters — portraits (Midjourney) and hex tokens (Gemini edit of the portrait)

## prompt

Full-figure portrait of {name}, a monster of {regionName} ({look}). {flavour}
Role: {role}. The creature is a casino thing made flesh: croupier, card, chip, dice, wheel or slot made cruel and
holy. Centred, three-quarter view, facing slightly left, lit by one candle and one {accentName} neon, feet or base
visible, isolated on plain near-black so it can be cut out. Square composition.

## variants

= *_token: From this portrait of {name}, make a hex-map head token: crop and repaint to a head-and-shoulders bust of this exact creature, same colours and lighting, centred in a square, isolated on plain near-black so it can be cut out. Painterly, readable at 26 px.

- boss_: Region boss of {regionName}: larger, crowned or enthroned, more gold, the neon stronger. Imposing silhouette that reads at a glance.
- the_litter: A raid boss: a heaving mass of many small things acting as one, in the gallows forest.
- high_table: A raid boss: the seated High Table of the Sky Citadel, six gilded figures as one creature.
- the_dealer: The final boss: a faceless Dealer on a throne that is a dealer's chair, cold gold, a visor where a face should be, long dealer's hands.
= dungheap: A destructible heap blocking a hex in a grimdark casino: a mound of broken chips, bone and ash, glinting with Gold nobody asks about, isolated on plain near-black so it can be cut out. Square composition.

## negative

cropped, cut off at the frame edge, busy background, scenery, multiple creatures (unless the name says so)
