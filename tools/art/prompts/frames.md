# frames — rarity frames as one 9-slice source per rarity (FLUX.2), sliced into six footprints by post.py

## prompt

Pixel-art ornamental frame border for the {rarity} rarity in a grimdark casino inventory: a square 192×192 frame
with a 36 px wide decorated border and a completely empty centre filled with flat pure green (#00FF00); the
outside beyond the frame is also flat pure green. Corners and edges decorated so they 9-slice cleanly (the
corners are 36×36, the edges repeat).

## variants

- _source_common: Common: plain tarnished brass, a few rivets.
- _source_rare: Rare: tarnished silver with a thin engraved line.
- _source_epic: Epic: worn gold with chip-and-dice relief in the corners.
- _source_legendary: Legendary: bone-white with hairline cracks and a faint inner light.

## negative

anything inside the frame, text, perspective, shadow, glow spilling outside
