# GM guide: your first night

Generated by `tools/build-gm-guide.mjs` from `data/balance.json`; numbers in this page are the live defaults. Ten minutes to read. The player rules are `docs/RULEBOOK.md`; every tunable is `docs/BALANCE.md`.

## Before people arrive (15 minutes)

1. On the PC that drives the TV, open **/gm**. The first visit asks you to set a GM PIN: it gates the console on every device, so pick something you can type on a phone.
2. **Create a room.** Pick the mode (Free-for-all, PvP only, PvE only, Teams, Royale), the difficulty tier (1 to 3; tier 1 for a table of new characters) and the size (Short, Regular, Long; Regular aims at about 90 minutes for 4 to 6 players, cap 8). The map, monsters and loot are rolled on the spot. You get a four-letter room code.
3. Press **Open TV**. It opens **/** as the shared screen in a new window; drag it to the TV and make it full screen. Click once on it so the browser allows sound. The TV shows the map, the room code and a QR code in a corner all night, so late arrivals and reconnects are one scan.
4. Everyone, you included, scans the QR with their phone camera. No install: the phone opens **/phone?code=XXXX** in the browser. Tell them to add it to the home screen if they like; it reconnects on its own if the screen locks.
5. Each phone picks a character from its roster or runs **creation**: look, class, then the stat gamble (10 chips, stakes of 1 to 5, 10:00 on the clock). Pull up to 3 items from the vault, pick a pet team, tap Ready. Players with no pets hatch one on the Triple Pet Wheel.
6. **Spotlight** (GM › Spotlight): pin up to four phones to the TV in a 2×2 grid so the table can watch someone gamble their Grit on Crash. It works the same way mid-run for fights.
7. **Buy-in ledger**: type each player's real buy-in for the night into the metagame ledger (GM › Ledger). It is a record, nothing more; the app never holds or moves money and the figure changes nothing in-game. Then either let each phone spin the **Buy-in Wheel** for its starting Health (80 to 150) or press **Flat start** for 100 across the board.
8. In Teams mode set the teams now (GM › Players › Teams). Press **Start**. Turn order is a shuffle shown on the TV.

## During play

You play from your own phone like everyone else; the console is for stepping in. Every prompt lands on the mover's phone, the TV only shows results. A turn is roll/spin/draw, step, resolve the hex, act, end; the chess clock adds 1:30 per turn, banks up to 5:00, and ends the turn at zero.

- **Pause** freezes every phone and every clock. Use it for pizza, not for arguments; arguments are what the undo is for.
- **A dead phone.** After 30 s of silence its prompts appear on the console as a **proxy** card; play the turn for them or press Skip. When the phone comes back the player picks up where the proxy left off. **Kick** removes a seat for good; **Reconnect** (GM › Players) hands a seat to a new device when someone swaps phones. **Autopilot** puts an absent player on the bot.
- **Rig a wheel** (GM › Rig): choose the stream (wheels, dice, loot, challenge, pets) and the result; the next draw from that stream lands there, animated so it looks fair. On the Challenge Wheel you see the result before the TV spins and can reroll or force it.
- **Challenges.** When two players spin the Challenge Wheel the table does not wait: their clocks pause and the turn order carries on around them. Hosted games report themselves. For a game played at the table or on Discord, both phones are asked who won and the hit lands when they agree; if they disagree, or when the 5:00 cap is close, press **Drop Back In**, pick the winner, and both players are returned to their spots. A timeout with no result costs both 10.
- **Undo** rolls back the last event; keep pressing for more. **Restore points** are named snapshots (GM › Restore); take one before anything you are nervous about, such as a raid. Restoring replays the room to that point for every device.
- **Config screen** (GM › Config): every number in `docs/BALANCE.md`, editable per room while playing. Change it, press Apply, every device follows. The Sheet import button reads your old GWF Config Sheet tabs into the same screen.
- **Other buttons you will use:** Broadcast (a message to every phone) and Whisper (to one), Grant/Take Gold and Health, Teleport, Spawn boss, Start Chaos tier, Take over monster (fight an encounter by hand), open any player's inventory, resize their grid, Reset Cartridge for a stuck character.

## The end

- The run ends on the mode's condition (throne, last one standing, final boss, last team) or when you press **End run** and name a winner. The TV shows the **recap**: standings, damage dealt and taken, kills, bosses, pets caught, the charts.
- Then the **vault** on each phone: survivors bank 3 items, the dead bank 1, and survivors may save their character as a **veteran** (it keeps level, stats and worn gear and is the way into tier 2 and 3). A dead character's name goes on the Wall of Names and can never be used again.
- The **Hall of Fame** (GM › Hall of Fame) updates on its own: runs cleared, deepest tier, kills, pets, the longest-lived character. The metagame ledger keeps one row per player per run with the buy-in you typed, the placing and what was vaulted, with season totals.
- Pets, the vault, veterans and the Hall of Fame persist; everything else is rolled again next time. A season is 8 nights.

## The classic fallback

If the backend is down or the Wi-Fi is hostile, the previous one-computer build is untouched at **/classic**: the GM presses every button, the TV window follows the GM window, nothing needs a phone. Its own guide is at /classic/gm/guide.html. Rooms and characters do not cross between the two.

## Troubleshooting

- **A phone cannot join.** Same Wi-Fi is not required (it goes through the backend), but the phone needs internet. Type the room code by hand at /phone if the camera will not read the QR. A phone that joined before shows a Reconnect banner; tap it.
- **The TV is behind.** The TV writes snapshots and back-fills gaps on its own; if it looks stuck, reload the TV tab. Nothing is lost: the room is the event log.
- **Sound is silent on the TV.** Browsers block audio until the window is clicked. Click the TV window once; GM › Sound test plays a cue.
- **Someone is stuck in a fight or a pool.** GM › Players › Reset Cartridge clears every effect on them and stands them on their last safe hex.
- **A result was wrong.** Undo it, or restore the last restore point. If a rule needs changing for the night, change the number in the config screen instead of arguing.
- **The room is gone.** Rooms live in the backend, not the browser; open /gm, enter the PIN, and pick the room from the list. Export the room as JSON (GM › Export) before you close the laptop if you want a copy.
