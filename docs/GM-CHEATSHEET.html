Gambling With Friends: RPG Edition — GM cheat sheet

Print both pages. You play from your own phone like everyone else; the PC console is for stepping in. Every number here is the live default (change any of them in GM › Config).

1 · Before people arrive (15 min)

  1. If Supabase has been idle a week it pauses — open the console the afternoon before and make sure a room loads.
  2. Open /gm on the PC that drives the TV. Set the GM PIN (you will type it on your phone too).
  3. Create a room: mode (Free-for-all for a first night), tier 1, size Regular, fog on. Note the room code.
  4. Open TV → drag the window to the TV, full screen, click it once so sound is allowed.
  5. Scan the QR with your own phone and join. Add it to the home screen.
  6. Ledger panel: type each player's real buy-in (a record only — the app never moves money).
  7. Teams mode only: set the teams in Table before Start.

2 · Creation (5–10 min)

  • Each phone: look → class → stat gamble (10 chips, stake 1–5 per game, 10:00 on the clock) → Lock in → vault pull → pets → Ready.
  • Spotlight (Rig & Spotlight) up to 4 phones on the TV so the room watches the gambles.
  • Returning players pick a veteran from their roster; a dead name is gone for good (Wall of Names).
  • Starting Health: let phones spin the Buy-in Wheel (80–150) or press Flat start (100). Then Start the run.

3 · A turn, in one line

Roll / spin / draw (your boots decide) → step up to the roll → resolve the hex → action (shop, bag, attack, challenge) → End turn. Clock: 1:30 per turn, banks to 5:00, ends the turn at zero; it stops while the monster acts.

The first lap is the tutorial. Watch the Proxy & Autopilot panel: it shows who the table is waiting on.

4 · When something happens

SituationPress
Phone dead / silent 30 sIts prompt appears on the console → Act for this phone (Proxy) or Skip. Back in play when the phone returns.
Someone leaves for goodAutopilot on their seat (bot plays), or Kick.
Swapped phonesTable › Reconnect hands the seat to the new device; or scan the QR, the seat is remembered by name.
PizzaPause — every phone and clock freezes.
Wrong result / argumentUndo (one event per press) or restore a Restore point. Take one before raids.
Rule feels offChange the number in Config → Apply. Do not argue, tune.
Want a dramatic spinRig: pick the stream (wheels, dice, loot, challenge, pets) and the result; the next draw lands there, animated.
Challenge dispute / stallChallenge lists › live card: Drop Back In → pick the winner. External games need both phones' "Who won?".
Stuck in a fight or poolTable › Reset Cartridge (clears effects, last safe hex).
Boss too tame / too meanMonsters: take over (play its hand yourself), re-spin its mood, move or spawn one.
Table is slowConfig → table.lastCallMinutes (default 100): the run ends on placings at that time.
Someone finds a bugThey tap 🐞 on their phone. Bugs & rewards → Reward 🎡: secret wheel on their phone only.

Any modeLast Call, or End run on the console

7 · The end of the night

  1. TV shows the recap; each phone picks its vault items and may save a veteran.
  2. Ledger & Recap: placings, buy-ins and what was vaulted, one row per player; Hall of Fame updates itself.
  3. Export the room JSON before closing the laptop (it has the whole night, bug reports included).
  4. Pets, vault, veterans and the Hall of Fame carry over; everything else is rolled fresh. A season is 8 nights.

8 · Troubleshooting

  • TV behind or frozen → reload the TV tab; nothing is lost, the room is the event log.
  • No sound → click the TV window once; Run › Sound test.
  • Phone can't join → needs internet (same Wi-Fi not required); type the code at /phone; tap the Reconnect banner if it joined before.
  • Room "gone" → /gm, enter the PIN, pick it from the list. Rooms live in the backend, not the browser.
  • Backend down → /classic on the PC; the GM presses every button, no phones.
