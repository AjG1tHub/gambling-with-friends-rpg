## How to read the lever columns

Every game has a governing stat and a lever (SPEC.md › Attacks and spells). The two stat columns are the same bot and the same number of rounds with the stat at its floor and at its ceiling; `test/unit/levers.test.mjs` checks every lever moves the return the promised way with paired dice. Threshold levers ("at 5", "at 6") switch on at that stat and, below neutral, trim win payouts by `items.leverPenaltyPerPoint` per point.

## Critical hits

A result at or above `topMult(gameId)` × `items.critXpMultiplierThreshold` is a crit (Blackjack: a natural; Mines: a full clear; Ride the Bus: all four calls). For binary games (50/50, War, Thimbles, Pai Gow) every win is the top multiplier, so every win is a crit — the spec says "the game's top multiplier is a critical hit"; the lead may want a per-game crit floor for those.
