🎛️ GM quickstart

Everything you need to run a night of Gambling With Friends. Ten minutes to read; keep the hotkey card next to the keyboard.

← GM monitor🧪 Game lab⌨ Hotkey card📖 Player rules

1 · Before people arrive

  1. On the computer that drives the TV, open the GM monitor (/gm) on your own screen and press Open Player View ↗. Drag that window to the TV and make it full screen (F11).
  2. Both windows must be in the same browser on the same computer — the shared screen follows the GM window directly. A phone or a second laptop will not see the game.
  3. Click once anywhere on the player view so the browser allows sound, then press ♪ test in the GM bar.
  4. Look at the chip next to ⚙ Settings: ✅ means the config is fine, ⚠️ / ⛔ means click it and read what it says.
  5. Add players (name, optional photo). Tick save and they are one click to seat next time — with their pets.
  6. Press Start Session.

2 · Running a turn

The highlighted button is always the next step. Ctrl+Alt+Space presses it for you, so a whole turn is usually four presses:

  1. Begin Turn — loans and the Collector tick, the turn clock starts.
  2. Spin Method — the wheel decides how they move.
  3. Move — the player calls directions; you press the arrow keys. (Some methods need no steps, or ask you to pick a player or a tile.)
  4. Resolve Tile — a minigame, a wheel, the shop or jail opens. Finish it.
  5. End Turn.

If two players end on the same tile the game offers a Battle. If someone is in jail, their turn opens the Jail panel instead.

3 · Minigames

  • Set the bet first (quick-bet buttons or type it). Players may bet other currencies — they are valued in Gold.
  • Follow the buttons in the game panel. Anything that plays out on the TV (cards, the Plinko ball, reels, the rocket) must finish before Settle lights up.
  • Settle is the only moment money moves. Until then you can still cancel the game.
  • Power-ups: Nudge, Reroll and Fork appear as extra buttons on chance games when the player holds one.
  • Live Odds (right column) changes any game's numbers for tonight only.
  • There are 36 games. To learn or test them without a session running, open the 🧪 Game lab: every game on one menu, a practice bankroll, nothing saved to the real table.

4 · When something goes wrong

I clicked the wrong thingCtrl+Alt+⌫ or ↩ Undo. One press = one click, up to 30 back. Balances, positions, wheels and pets all rewind together.
Someone's Gold is wrongGM Tools → pick the player, currency and amount → Apply. It is written to the ledger as a GM adjustment.
A player has to leave / arrives lateGM Tools → 🔀 Turn order / sit out / clock: sit a player out, bring them back, or change the order.
The TV froze or shows an old boardRefresh the player view. It redraws from the saved game; nothing is lost.
I closed the GM window / the browser crashedOpen /gm again — the session is saved after every click.
A red banner appeared at the bottomRead it. It offers Undo, Restore points and “Save session file”. Try the action once more; if it repeats, step back.
The whole table is wrong⚙ Settings → 🛟 Restore points. One is taken every round and every 5 minutes. Restoring can itself be undone.
We need to move to another computer⚙ Settings → Save session to a file, then Load session file on the other machine. History and saved players move from 🏆 History → export.
No soundClick once inside the player view (browsers block sound until then). Check 🔊 is ticked in the GM bar.

5 · The extras, in one line each

  • 🤝 Stakes — log a real-life side bet; it stays on the TV until you settle it. No Gold moves.
  • 🦈 Loan Shark (Ctrl+Alt+L) — lend, repay, see who is overdue.
  • 💸 Buy-in (Ctrl+Alt+U) — spins the Buy-in wheel and carries out the result.
  • 🔨 Auction — put a player's currencies up for bids; call “going once / twice / sold”.
  • 🐾 Pets (Ctrl+Alt+Y) — hatch, train, heal, equip. Pet battle (Ctrl+Alt+F) — boss fights, player vs player, raids.
  • Chaos (Ctrl+Alt+1–5) — shuffle the board, loudly. Add Shift to do it silently. Ctrl+Alt+Z takes the last shuffle back.
  • 📒 Ledger — every event tonight, filterable, with a CSV download.
  • ⏱ Turn timer — optional shot clock on the TV (same panel as the turn order). It only nags; it never ends a turn by itself.

6 · Ending the night

  1. Press End Session. The TV shows the recap: standings, awards, a Gold chart and the all-time table.
  2. The result is added to the 🏆 Hall of Fame and saved players keep their pets and Power.
  3. For next time: ⚙ Settings → Wipe session clears the table (history and saved players stay).

7 · Changing the rules

Every number — odds, wheel slices, prices, pet stats — lives in the Google Sheet “GWF Config”. Edit the Sheet, then press Ctrl+Alt+R in the GM window. The config chip tells you straight away if something was mistyped, and the player rules page updates itself.

Without a Sheet connected the game uses its built-in rules.
