Repeats for every player, every round

One turn

  1. ●Begin turnBuilt and auto-testedCollector and loans tick. Skipped turns are burned here.
  2. ●In jail? Jail turn insteadBuilt and auto-testedJumps to the Jail block below.
  3. ●Spin the Method WheelBuilt and auto-tested11 ways to move.
  4. ●MoveBuilt and auto-testedPlayer calls directions, GM presses arrows.
  5. ●LandBuilt and auto-testedOn another player: Battle. Otherwise the tile resolves.
  6. ●End turnBuilt and auto-testedRound counter, rank arrows, shot clock restarts.

Ways to move

  1. ●Dice · D4 · Double Dice · Move OneBuilt and auto-tested
  2. ●Candy LandBuilt and auto-testedWalk to a colour. Now gives up after two laps if the colour is gone.
  3. ●Teleport · Magnetism · Stay PutBuilt and auto-tested
  4. ●Swap · BenevolenceBuilt and auto-testedPick another player.
  5. ●The Long WalkBuilt and auto-testedWalk until you bump into someone.
  6. ●Edge crossing → the VoidBuilt and auto-tested20% per crossing; 9 outcomes.
  7. ●Shot clockBuilt and auto-testedOptional. Only nags, never ends a turn.
